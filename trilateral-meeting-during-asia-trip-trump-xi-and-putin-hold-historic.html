Trilateral Meeting During Asia Trip Trump, Xi and Putin Hold Historic

Leaders discuss global security, nuclear risks, international cooperation and economic relations during a landmark summit in China.

By News Desk November 20, 2026

U.S. President Donald Trump’s latest diplomatic trip to Asia has drawn international attention following an unprecedented trilateral meeting with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen, China.

The meeting took place on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit on November 19, 2026. The three leaders discussed several major international issues, including nuclear risks, global security, economic cooperation and relations between their countries.

Leaders Issue Joint Declaration

Following the meeting, the three countries released a joint declaration outlining areas of cooperation. The statement included commitments to support reform of the United Nations, strengthen international dialogue and discuss ways to reduce nuclear risks and prevent the spread of nuclear weapons.

However, the declaration did not establish a specific timeline for nuclear arms reduction negotiations. It also did not directly address Russia’s ongoing war in Ukraine.

Trump Describes Meeting as Successful

Speaking to NBC News after the meeting, Trump described the discussions positively and said that strong relationships had been built between the leaders.

He also indicated that discussions with Putin included Russia’s war in Ukraine. Trump expressed hope that progress could be made toward ending the conflict.

International Reactions and Concerns

The meeting has attracted different reactions from political leaders and international observers. Some have highlighted the importance of direct diplomatic engagement, while others have questioned whether the summit will produce meaningful long-term agreements.

U.S. Senate Democratic leader Chuck Schumer criticized the meeting, describing it as a historic embarrassment. His comments reflected concerns among some American lawmakers about the diplomatic implications of the summit.

Trump Expected to Address Reporters

After returning from Asia, Trump announced plans to hold a news conference at the White House on Friday, November 20. He was expected to discuss his meetings with Xi and Putin, as well as trade agreements involving Thailand, the Philippines and other countries.

The outcome of the summit and the commitments made by the three governments are likely to remain important subjects in international diplomacy.
